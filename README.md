**NurseToolBox**
NurseToolBox is a set of study and bedside tools for nursing students and nurses, made by Ralph Marco Alonzo. Each app takes a core nursing skill, such as concept mapping, ABG reading, IV math, OB dating, staffing or research planning, and turns it into a step-by-step calculator or guide. Every app shows its work and cites its sources, so you can check each answer and learn how it was reached.

**NurseMap**
Worked concept maps for 35 common medical-surgical diseases. Each map follows the disease from risk factors and pathophysiology to signs and symptoms, diagnostics, management and nursing diagnoses. Use it to study for exams or to guide your own care plans.

**NurseABG**
A step-by-step guide to reading arterial blood gases. Enter the pH, PaCO2 and HCO3 values, and the app walks you through the acid-base disorder and whether it is compensated. It also has a bedside guide and a quiz to practice on.

**NurseResearch Pro**
A planner for nursing research. It takes you from the research problem through the design, sampling and methods to a full study blueprint. It also helps you choose the right statistical test for your data.

**NurseIV**
Quick, checkable IV math for the bedside. It covers drip rates by drop factor, medication drips, dose volumes and an IV fluids reference. Every result shows the formula it used so you can double-check it, and a quiz helps you practice.

**NurseOB**
Obstetric dating made simple. Compute the expected date of delivery with Naegele's rule, find the age of gestation, and score a newborn with the New Ballard Score. A quiz helps you review.

**NurseLead**
A staffing calculator for nurse leaders and managers. It computes nursing staff needs from patient classification and nursing care hours, checks the result against DBM staffing ratios, and builds a shift rost
